THE UPDATE DROP

Hold 1%. Pick your heists.

Once VI is out and the official locations and heists are mapped, they come to Heistpad as new coins. Anyone holding a combined 1% or more of Heistpad coins gets airdropped that same share of supply in the new locations, and you choose which ones.

The rules

  1. Combined 1% minimum. Add up your share of each coin's supply. 0.5% of $NEOPALMS + 0.3% of $PAD39 + 0.2% of $MACCA = 1%. You're in.
  2. Snapshot. Taken the moment the official map update goes live, and announced 48 hours ahead. Selling before the snapshot means you're out.
  3. Your share carries over. For every location or heist you pick, you get the same % of its supply as your combined holding, capped at 10% per location.
  4. Picks. One pick per full 1% you hold, up to 10 picks. 1% = 1 pick, 3% = 3 picks, 7.4% = 7 picks. You get 72 hours to choose.
  5. Hot spots. If too many holders pick the same location, its airdrop is capped at 30% of supply and split in proportion to what each holder asked for.
  6. Who's left out. Liquidity pools, the Vault, and team and platform wallets don't count toward the snapshot.
