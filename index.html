      07 · Update Drop

      Hold 1%. Pick your heists.

      GTA VI is out. As the official locations and heists get mapped, they come to Heistpad as new coins. Anyone holding a combined 1% or more of Heistpad coins gets airdropped that same share of supply in the new locations, and you choose which ones.

      1. Combined 1% minimum. Add up your share of each coin's supply. 0.5% + 0.3% + 0.2% across three coins = 1%. You're in.
      2. Snapshot. Announced 48 hours ahead. Selling before the snapshot means you're out.
      3. Your share carries over. For every location you pick, you get the same % of its supply as your combined holding, capped at 10% per location.
      4. Picks. One pick per full 1% you hold, up to 10. You get 72 hours to choose.
      5. Hot spots. If too many holders pick the same location, its airdrop is capped at 30% of supply and split in proportion.
      6. Left out. Liquidity pools, the Vault, and team and platform holdings don't count.
